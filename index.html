FAQ

Straight answers.

Where are you and what are the hours?

3056 S State Rd 7 Ste 78, Miramar, FL 33023. Open Tuesday to Saturday, 9am–6pm. Serving Miami, Miami Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Weston, Hollywood and Pembroke Pines. Tap Message the Mechanic to text, WhatsApp or call.

Do you do dyno tuning and ECU work?

Yes — in-house dyno tuning, ECU flashing, exhaust and top-end performance work for sport bikes, V-twins and metric bikes.

What brands do you service?

Harley-Davidson, Ducati, BMW Motorrad, Suzuki, Yamaha, Kawasaki, Honda, Aprilia, MV Agusta and most other makes — independent, without dealership rates.

Do you handle crash estimates and collision work?

Yes. Crash estimates, frame and fork inspection, and collision repair for riders and insurance work across South Florida.

What should I send before bringing a bike in?

Year, make, model, mileage, the exact symptom, when it happens, recent work, and a short video if sound or a dash warning matters.

Do you serve Miami and Fort Lauderdale?

Yes. We are in Miramar, central to Miami-Dade and Broward, and riders come to us from across South Florida.
