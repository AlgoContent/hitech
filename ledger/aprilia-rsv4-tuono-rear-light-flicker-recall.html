What is actually happening

How a tech diagnoses it

You do not start by taking things apart. You start by confirming you are chasing the right problem on the right bike. Here is the order it actually gets worked, step by step.

  1. Verify VIN eligibility
  2. Confirm flicker or intermittent lamp function
  3. Replace both rear turn-signal assemblies
  4. Verify normal running-light and turn-signal operation
  5. Confirm no key-off drain complaint

The fix

The repair the manufacturer specifies is direct: Replace the left- and right-hand rear turn signals under Aprilia recall 25V591. This is documented under 25V591, sourced from Aprilia safety recall / NHTSA. That matters for two reasons. First, on a recall the parts and labor are usually covered, so you are not paying out of pocket for a factory defect. Second, the corrected part is an updated design — it is not the same component going back in to fail again on the same schedule.
